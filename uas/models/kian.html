Kian family

Photo sourcesModel description

Kian is used for publicly presented unmanned-aircraft designs with numbered variants. A family label should not erase differences between what was actually displayed.

Names and aliases
Kian-1 · Kian-2 · Kiyan

Variant names retained for literature searches; morphology and current inventory are not independently resolved here.

Description cues

  • Use a numbered label only with an attributable image or document.
  • Keep distinct displays and dates separate in a reference file.

Confusions and limits

  • Kian and Arash relationships are described inconsistently in public reporting.
  • Do not infer propulsion or external geometry from a bare family name.

Understand the design vocabulary

Unresolved configuration
This entry does not establish one airframe layout. Keep the original source label and distinguish the pictured object from any claimed family relationship.
Variant
A particular version within a family. A shared name or number does not establish a shared structure.

These terms describe broad structures. They do not authenticate a model label, origin, operator, or current use.

Work through a public source

1 · Attribute

Write “the source calls this…” and retain the original title, creator, and stated date. Distinguish a photograph from an exhibit, drawing, or illustration.

2 · Describe

Use broad vocabulary for visible structures and viewpoint. Keep the caption’s claim separate from observations supported by the image.

3 · Qualify

List unresolved attribution, variant, capture date, provenance, or obscured surfaces. A reuse license does not verify the aircraft label.
