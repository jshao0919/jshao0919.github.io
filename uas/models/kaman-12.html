Understand the design vocabulary

Wing and tail
The main wing and tail surfaces are different structural elements. Describe only what the view resolves; an obscured tail is not evidence that no tail exists.
Viewpoint
Plan, side, and oblique views reveal different surfaces. A source caption and a visible feature are separate kinds of information.

These terms describe broad structures. They do not authenticate a model label, origin, operator, or current use.

Work through a public source

1 · Attribute

Write “the source calls this…” and retain the original title, creator, and stated date. Distinguish a photograph from an exhibit, drawing, or illustration.

2 · Describe

Use broad vocabulary for visible structures and viewpoint. Keep the caption’s claim separate from observations supported by the image.

3 · Qualify

List unresolved attribution, variant, capture date, provenance, or obscured surfaces. A reuse license does not verify the aircraft label.
