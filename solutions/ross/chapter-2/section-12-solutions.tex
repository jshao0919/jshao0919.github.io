\section{Limit superior and limit inferior}
\label{sec:limsup-liminf}
For each integer $N\ge0$, the tail after $N$ is the nonempty set
\[
 E_N=\{s_n:n>N\}.
\]
Definition 10.6 gives
\[
 \limsup s_n=\lim_{N\to\infty}\sup E_N,\qquad
 \liminf s_n=\lim_{N\to\infty}\inf E_N.
\]
These limits may be infinite. When a tail bound is infinite, we
check the case through the definitions and extended order before
applying any theorem about sequences of real numbers.

A tail supremum need not be attained by a term. Likewise, a term
may lie above the lim sup or below the lim inf at arbitrarily
large indices. The solutions check the relevant bound and
eventuality conditions explicitly.
