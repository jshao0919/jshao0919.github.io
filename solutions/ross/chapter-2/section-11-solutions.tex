\section{Subsequences}
\label{sec:subsequences}
A subsequence has the form $(s_{n_k})_{k\in\N}$, where its original
indices satisfy $n_1<n_2<\cdots$. The selection map is
$\sigma(k)=n_k$. The index $k$ counts selected terms, whereas $n_k$
is their position in the original sequence.

Subsequential limits in this section may be real numbers or
$+\infty$ or $-\infty$. A proposed list must account for every
possible subsequential limit. If $S$ denotes the set of those limits,
we use
\[
 \limsup s_n=\sup S,\qquad \liminf s_n=\inf S,
\]
as established in Theorem 11.8. Increasing and decreasing remain
weak notions, so a constant subsequence is monotone.
