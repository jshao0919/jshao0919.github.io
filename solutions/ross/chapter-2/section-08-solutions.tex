\section{A discussion about proofs}
\label{sec:direct-limit-proofs}
Every limit proof in this section starts with an arbitrary
$\varepsilon>0$, chooses an appropriate threshold, and verifies the
required inequality for every integer index beyond that threshold.
An exploratory rearrangement helps discover $N$; the formal argument
then runs forward from $n>N$. Divergence proofs instead show that
the definition cannot hold for any proposed real limit.
No limit law from Section 9 is assumed.
