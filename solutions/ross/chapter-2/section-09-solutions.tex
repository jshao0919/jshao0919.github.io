\section{Limit theorems for sequences}
\label{sec:limit-theorems}
This section proves and applies rules for limits. Each application checks
the hypotheses: the sequences must have the required finite limits,
and a quotient needs a nonzero limiting denominator.

Definition 9.8 extends the phrase \emph{a limit exists} to include divergence
to $+\infty$ or $-\infty$. These are not finite real limits.
We use the extended convention where the exercise permits it, while
checking separately whether a finite-limit theorem applies.
A recurrence's fixed-point equation gives a necessary condition for
a finite limit; it does not prove convergence.
