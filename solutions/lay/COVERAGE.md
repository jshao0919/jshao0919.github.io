# Coverage of the supplied Lay textbook

The collection follows the fourth edition (2012), printed Chapters 1–8.
It includes every numbered exercise, all subparts, and all practice problems
in the 58 sections and seven chapter supplementary sets. The appendices
have no numbered exercise sets. Online Chapters 9–10 require separate source text.
