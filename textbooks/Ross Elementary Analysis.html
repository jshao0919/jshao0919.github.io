Ross — Elementary Analysis (Solutions)

← Back to Mathjock

Detailed worked solutions for Kenneth A. Ross, Elementary Analysis: The Theory of Calculus, second edition (2013). Every exercise and subpart in Chapter 1, Sections 1–6 is covered: 56 exercises in total, including the enrichment section on Dedekind cuts. Chapter 2, Sections 7–16 is also complete: 117 exercises on limits, monotone and Cauchy sequences, subsequences, lim sup/lim inf, metric-space topology, series, convergence tests, and decimal expansions. Sections 17–20 of Chapter 3 add 60 exercises on continuity, extreme and intermediate values, uniform continuity, and limits of functions, including the requested graph sketches. The new continuation adds all 214 exercises in Sections 21–38, completing coverage of every exercise and subpart across the book’s 38 sections.

Each proof starts with its goal and explains the intermediate algebra, assumptions, and logical steps. The solutions are typeset in LaTeX. Exercise numbering follows the textbook PDF already on this site.

Sections 21–38: a step-by-step continuation

Written for students beginning undergraduate analysis, these solutions explain what each problem asks, how to choose a proof strategy, and why each algebraic step or theorem applies. Read the section introduction first, then try a problem before consulting its worked solution. Any printed ambiguities or errors in the text are identified in the relevant solution.
